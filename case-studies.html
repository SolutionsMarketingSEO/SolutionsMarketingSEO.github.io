Montreal Dental Clinic | Technical Audit

Demonstration audit · Bilingual clinic website

Health Score Found: 40 / 100

The situation

A modern clinic website where form outran structure. Dozens of images, animated sections, and no sitemap for search engines to follow. Solid brand, weak foundations.

What the audit found

  • Meta descriptions missing entirely on roughly a third of pages
  • Multiple H1 headings per page on 8 pages, diluting each page's stated topic
  • Over 25 pages carrying images without alt text
  • No sitemap.xml found, forcing search engines to guess the page structure

Crawled page by page across both languages, the way a search engine discovers a site without a map.
